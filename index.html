Accuracy notice

This browser prototype is not a certified sound-level meter. iPhone microphone sensitivity, automatic gain control, noise suppression, case/hand placement and frequency response can affect readings. The A/C weighting filters and spectrum are intended as an engineering prototype, not proof of compliance with IEC 61672. Do not use it for legal noise compliance or hearing-safety decisions without calibration and validation against a suitable meter.

Tip: for better event measurements, use a supported calibrated external measurement microphone/interface and verify the full signal path. Keep the phone away from loudspeaker airflow and do not cover its microphones.
